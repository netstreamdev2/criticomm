A medical call, from first ring to closed case

Follow one medical call through HikTech. A call logger takes it, the EMS dispatch desk sends an ambulance, a paramedic accepts on the phone, treats the patient, takes them to hospital and hands them over to a doctor, files the patient care report, and a case manager closes the case.

Step 1 · Paramedic · phone

Signing on to an ambulance

Five steps, once a shift. An ambulance with a fault, or without its stock, does not go out.

Step 2 · Call logger · console

Logging the call

The same call form as every department. For a medical call, the triage box matters most.

Step 3 · EMS dispatcher · console

Dispatching an ambulance

The EMS desk sees only calls sent to EMS and only EMS crews on duty, nearest first with their ambulance and minutes away. It sends to all, or assigns one paramedic, exactly as the other desks do.

Step 4 · Paramedic · phone

Accept, treat, transport

The same pop-up and route as every crew. Then the ladder: En Route, On Scene, and Transporting when the patient is in the ambulance.

  1. 1File patient report is ready from On Scene; the draft can be started at any time and is kept on the phone.
  2. 2Mark Transporting when leaving for hospital. The dispatcher sees it at once.

Step 5 · Paramedic and receiving doctor · phone

The patient care report and the handover

The electronic PCR replaces the paper form. Sections open one at a time; only a few things are required, the rest are filled as the call allows.

  1. 1Eight sections, from the patient’s details to the vitals (blood pressure, pulse, oxygen, glucose, GCS, pain) and every drug given with its time, dose and route. Required: the care level (BLS, ILS or ALS), the chief complaint and the patient’s response to treatment.
  2. 2More than one patient? Each extra casualty is added with a triage colour: Green, Yellow, Red or Black.
  3. 3Handover at hospital. The receiving doctor’s name, the patient’s condition, and both signatures, drawn on the paramedic’s phone. The handover is kept as its own record.
  4. 4Photos and voice notes from the call go with the report.
  5. 5Submit. The report is numbered PCR-INC-20261013-0011. EMS’s part of the call is finished. With signal missing, it is saved and sends itself later.

Step 6 · Automatic

After the call

Stock used up

Anything ticked as short in the restock checklist is sent to EMS command: Ambulance stock short: AMB-07.

Needs cleaning

If the ambulance needs cleaning, it is taken off the road and command is told. The crew signs onto another one.

Hospital handovers

Every handover is listed on the console (Hospital Handovers), newest first, filtered by hospital. The receiving doctor can read them.

Step 7 · Case manager · console

Closing the case

The call is Resolved once every department on it has finished. The case manager sees Awaiting review · Patient care report PCR-INC-20261013-0011 by P. Pillay, reads the report, evidence and history, adds an optional note, and closes it. If Police or Fire were also on the call, each files its own report and the case closes when all are in.
