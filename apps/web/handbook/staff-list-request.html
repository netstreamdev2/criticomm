The staff list we need

To give every member of the emergency services their own HikTech account, we need one up-to-date list of the people who will use it. This page explains what goes in the list, what stays out of it, and what happens once you send it.

For: eThekwini HR, with the department administrators Comes with: HikTech staff list template.xlsx Updated: 4 October 2026

Who to include

One row for each person who works in the emergency services and will use HikTech, at a desk or in the field:

  • the control room: call takers, dispatchers and their supervisors
  • Metro Police, Fire & Rescue, EMS / Medical and Disaster Management, every rank
  • current staff only: people who have left should not be on the list

Being on the list gives nobody access to anything. Each person later receives their own invitation and activates their own account.

Please leave out

  • ID numbers
  • Dates of birth
  • Home addresses
  • Personal email addresses
  • Salaries and pay details
  • Medical or disciplinary records

HikTech does not need any of these, and the Protection of Personal Information Act asks us to hold no more than the purpose needs. If your export has other columns, you can leave them in: the import ignores every column it does not recognise. Those columns never leave the administrator’s computer and are never stored.

Why each item is needed

  • Name and badge number identify the person, and the badge number is how they prove it is them when they activate their account.
  • Rank, department and station decide which screens they see and which incidents reach them.
  • Work email or cellphone carries their invitation. Without either, a printed slip does.
  • Role in the current system saves administrators setting each role from scratch.

The list is held in HikTech’s staff register. Administrators manage it. Colleagues who coordinate emergency response, such as dispatchers and supervisors, can see names, ranks, stations and work contact details, so they can reach the people on duty. It is used for accounts and for running the service, nothing else.

Getting it right first time

  • Each badge number belongs to one person. Two people with the same badge number cannot both be imported.
  • Keep the template’s headings as they are, in the first row. An export from another system works too, as long as its columns carry these headings or common ones such as “First name”, “Surname”, “Force number”, “Dept” or “Cell number”.
  • Save it as an Excel workbook (.xlsx) or CSV. Older .xls files need saving again as .xlsx.
  • One file for everyone is easiest, but a department or a station at a time works too.

What happens next

  1. The administrator checks it on screen

    The file is read on their own computer and nothing is saved until they confirm. Any row with a problem, such as a missing badge number, is shown to them, and may come back to you to correct.

  2. Administrators confirm roles

    An administrator, usually the department’s own, confirms the role each person will have. Administrator rights are never taken from the list; they are given individually and approved by a second person.

  3. Each person activates their own account

    They receive an invitation by email, SMS or printed slip, type their badge number, choose their own password and accept the privacy notice. Nobody else ever sees or sets their password.

  4. Every month, a fresh list

    An updated list keeps the register in step. New people are invited. People no longer on the list are shown to the administrators to review before anything changes; nobody is removed automatically.

Sending it back

Return the file to the HikTech administrator who sent you this request, through the municipality’s usual secure channel rather than personal email. If your policy needs a password on the file, send the password separately; the administrator removes it before importing.

Questions about a column, or about someone who does not fit neatly, are best asked before sending. The administrator who sent the request can answer them.
