A disaster call, from first ring to closed case

Follow one flooding call through HikTech. A call logger sends it to Disaster Management, the Disaster desk sends an officer and records the resources deployed, the officer goes to the scene and files the After-Action Report, and a case manager closes the case.

Step 1 · Disaster officer · phone

Going on duty

Two steps. Disaster officers sign onto no vehicle, so there is no inspection.

Step 2 · Call logger · console

Logging the call

The same form as every call: caller, number, patient question, map pin and region, then the details. For a flood, the Disaster group of incident codes (60-1 Flooding) suggests Disaster Management.

Step 3 · Disaster desk · console

Dispatch, and the resources deployed

The Disaster desk dispatches its officers from the queue, exactly like the other desks. It also records what is being sent to the scene.

The Disaster dashboard

Active incidents, people displaced, average response time and resources dispatched; a map of the active incidents; current Durban wind and weather; and the deployment monitor, where the desk logs a periodic update for each deployment. The mutual-aid partner directory lists contacts and radio channels for joint operations.

Step 4 · Disaster officer · phone

At the scene

The same pop-up as every crew: Accept incident, Acknowledge or Navigate. Then En Route and On Scene, with photos, voice notes and updates. Once On Scene, the button becomes File post-mortem.

Step 5 · Officer · phone, or command · console

The After-Action Report

Short and focused on impact. The officer leading Disaster Management’s part files it from the scene; a Disaster supervisor or administrator can also file it from the console for a deployed incident.

  1. 1When it started and when it was resolved. The duration is worked out; the end time can’t be in the future or before the start.
  2. 2Impact: injuries, deaths, and people displaced who need shelter. Whole numbers only.
  3. 3Root cause and lessons learned are required, with the reporting officer’s name.
  4. 4The call’s timeline and the desk’s deployment updates are attached to the report as they stood.
  5. 5File. Numbered PM-INC-20261013-0015, it finishes Disaster Management’s part of the call. Command can still correct it until the case is closed.

Step 6 · Case manager · console

Closing the case

When every department on the call has finished (Disaster Management, and Fire and EMS if they were sent), the call is Resolved. The case manager reads the reports, evidence and history and closes the case. Every post-mortem is also listed on the console under Post-mortem reports and in the case PDF.
