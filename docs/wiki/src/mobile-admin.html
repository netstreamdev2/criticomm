Alerts (tab)Inbox with a live unread badge and acknowledgement state.

Two overlays sit above every screen: the SOS button (Chapter 32) and the sync tray (Chapter 31).

29.2 The duty gate

You cannot open incidents until today's readiness flow is complete. On opening the app with it outstanding, you are routed to the Shift tab.

Two deliberate escapes Profile stays reachable — somebody who fails the readiness check can never satisfy the gate, which is correct, but must still be able to see who they are signed in as and sign out. A gate with no exit is a trap, not a control. And if the check cannot be evaluated because the device is offline, you are let through: the server remains the record of truth, and stranding a crew behind a check we cannot run helps nobody.
Chapter 30

Mobile Task Procedures

30.1 Sign in
Screen Login
  1. Enter your Criticomm email and password — the same credentials as the console.
  2. The session persists, so you will not normally sign in again during a tour.
If it hangs on "Restoring your session" the app has failed through to login rather than trapping you. Sign in again.
30.2 Work an assigned call
Screen Incidents tab → detail
  1. Open the call from the Incidents tab. Critical calls carry a red strip: Priority 1 — respond immediately.
  2. Navigate hands the coordinates to the phone's own map application for turn-by-turn.
  3. Call caller dials the number on the incident.
  4. Advance the status with the single forward button as each thing happens.
  5. Add timeline updates and evidence while on scene.
30.3 See what else is live nearby
Screen Incidents tab → Nearby
  1. Switch to Nearby.
  2. Allow location access to sort by distance; without it you still get the list, unsorted.
Read-only You cannot advance the status of a call assigned to another crew. Situational awareness, not a second dispatch desk.
30.4 Capture evidence
Screen incident detail → Scene evidence
  1. Camera — take a photograph. Library — attach an existing one.
  2. Record voice note — tap to start; the control turns red and counts. Tap again to stop and file it.
Coverage is irrelevant Files are copied out of the OS cache into storage the app controls, then uploaded when there is signal. A photograph of a scene that has since been cleared cannot be retaken, which is why it is retained rather than left in the cache.
30.5 Acknowledge an alert
Screen Alerts tab, or the full-screen interrupt
  1. A High or Critical dispatch opens a full-screen alert. Choose Acknowledge & open to go straight to the call, or Acknowledge to clear it.
  2. Anything you missed is in the Alerts tab. Opening an unread alert there acknowledges it if acknowledgement was required.
  3. Mark all read clears the badge.
The banner at the top of the inbox counts alerts still waiting on your acknowledgement.
30.6 Apply for leave
Screen Profile → Apply for leave
  1. Check your balances at the top.
  2. Choose a type, set the dates as YYYY-MM-DD, add a reason.
  3. Submit. The summary line tells you how many days and what would remain — and warns if it exceeds your balance.
Exceeding your balance does not block submission. Your supervisor decides.
30.7 Ask Criti
Screen Profile → Ask Criti
  1. Ask about your own calls, your crew or your shift.
  2. Suggested questions are offered on the empty screen.
Scope Criti sees exactly what you can see. If a question fails, the text is put back in the box so you need not retype it one-handed.
Chapter 31

The Offline Outbox

Every field action is written to the phone before it is sent. This is why a dead zone costs you nothing, and why you should never press a button twice.

31.1 What you see

IndicatorMeaning
Waiting to send on a cardYour change is recorded on the phone and will deliver itself.
Sync pill above the tab barCount of undelivered items. Amber when offline, red when something failed.
Sync tray (tap the pill)Every undelivered item, when it was captured, how many attempts, and the error if it failed.

31.2 What to do

  1. Nothing, normally. The queue delivers on reconnect, on returning to the app, and on a timer.
  2. Before signing out, check the tray is empty. Work is held against your account — if you sign out with items queued they wait for you, not for the next person carrying the handset.
  3. If something shows as failed, open the tray and read the error. Try again now retries everything.
  4. Discard only if you are certain the item is no longer needed. It is deleted and never reaches the case file.

31.3 What the queue guarantees

GuaranteeConsequence for you
Honest timesThe recorded time is when you pressed the button, not when signal returned.
Nothing droppedAn item that cannot be delivered is parked and stays visible until a human deals with it.
Safe replayIf delivery is interrupted mid-flight, the retry cannot create a duplicate.
Emergency firstA panic is delivered before a queue of photographs.
Your work onlyItems are delivered under the account that captured them.
Retries only count when you have signal The queue refuses to attempt delivery while the device is offline, so the retry budget is not spent driving through a tunnel. Eight failures means the server is rejecting the write — a real problem worth reporting.
Chapter 32

Officer-Down

Call for emergency assistance
Control the SOS button, on every screen  ·  Procedure raise_panic()
  1. Press and hold the SOS button. It reads HOLD… and fills.
  2. The phone taps you at each third of the hold, so you can feel it arming without looking.
  3. After 2.5 seconds it fires. Release earlier and nothing happens.
  4. A confirmation tells you whether your position was captured.
What happens A Critical incident is created at your position and a blocking alert is raised to your own department and the control room. With no signal it is queued at the highest priority and sent the moment you have any. Position is raced against a four-second timeout — if no fix is available it sends anyway, marked position unknown.
Use the radio too The platform is a second channel, not a replacement. Raise it on the radio as well if you can.

If you trigger it accidentally: say so on the radio, and ask dispatch to close the incident with a note. There is no cancel — the button is deliberately easy to trigger and impossible to un-send, because hesitation is the worse failure.

Chapter 33

Alerting and Push

33.1 Checking a handset before a shift

Open Profile → Dispatch alerts. It states exactly one of:

MessageMeaning and action
Push enabledThe handset will ring for a dispatch even locked. Nothing to do.
Notifications blockedPermission was declined. Fixable here — turn notifications on for Criticomm in device settings.
Push not in this buildThis build has no push service configured. Not fixable on the device — report it; the handset should not go out on shift.
Push unavailable on this deviceAn emulator or simulator. Expected in testing.
Push registration failedSomething went wrong registering. Report it for checking.

In every one of these cases, alerts still arrive instantly while the app is open.

33.2 Why there are several channels

Realtime covers open apps; push covers locked phones; a local notification covers an alert arriving while the app is backgrounded; the inbox covers everything afterwards. Each alone has a blind spot, and a missed dispatch is not recoverable — so they overlap deliberately, and duplicates are deduplicated by alert id.

Configuration

Admin → Configuration holds the lists the service can change without a deployment. Everything here is soft-deleted — toggled inactive rather than removed — so historical records stay interpretable.

35.1 Add a configuration record
Screen /admin/config  ·  Writes command_records
  1. Choose the module: Incident Category, Leave Type, Shift Type, Report Template or Ops Link.
  2. Enter the name and code.
  3. For an incident sub-category, set the parent group; leaving it blank makes this row a group.
  4. For a category, add extra form prompts, one per line, to appear on the log-call screen.
  5. Save.
Leave types carry entitlements. The annual day allowance lives on the leave-type record, which is what the balances on the leave screens are computed against.
35.2 Repoint an external ops link
Screen /admin/config, module OpsLink
  1. Find the link — E-NATIS, CAS, PERSAL, FIREARM, FINES, TRACE, FORENSICS and the rest.
  2. Set its endpoint.
Until an endpoint is issued the link renders dimmed in the command bar rather than looking broken.
35.3 Retire a configuration record
Screen /admin/config
  1. Toggle the record inactive.
It stops being offered on capture forms but existing records that reference it still read correctly.

Operations Settings

Twelve knobs at /admin/operations. Each declares a type, bounds and a default; values are clamped on read and write, every change is audited, and if the table is unreachable the code defaults apply — a tuning problem can never take a screen down.

SettingEffect
sla.clock_warn_secondsWhen the intake clock on the log-call screen turns amber.
sla.clock_critical_secondsWhen it turns red.
sla.response_target_minutesThe response target the dashboard gauge measures against.
sla.call_duration_target_minutesThe call-handling target.
alerts.modal_min_severityWhich severity interrupts with a blocking pop-up. The single most operationally significant knob here.
alerts.sound_enabledWhether an alert plays a tone.
alerts.toast_secondsHow long a corner toast stays.
dispatch.eta_speed_kmhAssumed speed in the ETA model.
dispatch.eta_road_factorRoad-distance multiplier over straight line.

board.feed_itemsHow many items the ops board feed shows.
board.volume_daysThe window the volume chart covers.
Change a setting
Screen /admin/operations
  1. Edit the value and save. If it is out of bounds it is clamped, and the field updates to show what was actually stored.
  2. Reset removes the override and returns to the built-in default.
Raising alerts.modal_min_severity is an operational decision Set it to Critical and High-severity dispatches stop interrupting crews. That may be right for a busy department, but it changes what people are pulled away from other work for. Record why.
Chapter 37

Automations

37.1 What runs

Seven rules in the database on a five-minute tick. Everything Criticomm did before the current release happened because a person did it — right for dispatching, wrong for noticing that a call has been waiting twenty minutes or a licence lapses next week.

37.2 The rules

RuleEveryBehaviour and defaults
Unassigned call escalation5 minHigh alert at 10 minutes waiting; Critical blocking interrupt at 20. To the owning department and the control room.
No arrival recorded10 minDispatched or En Route with no arrival after 30 minutes.
Open incident gone quiet30 minActive incident with no timeline entry for 4 hours. At most one nudge per incident per day.
Certification expiry12 hWarns at 30, 7 and 1 days — to the member and the department.
Unacknowledged duty1 hPublished duty starting within 12 hours that the member has not confirmed.
Morning digest30 minOnce per department per day at the configured hour: volume, criticals, closure rate, average response.
Housekeeping24 h / 6 hPrunes expired alerts, old run logs, spent suppression records and stale rate-limit counters.

37.3 Managing them

Tune, disable or run a rule
Screen /admin/automations  ·  Who Admin tier  ·  Audited
  1. Read the health banner first. It says whether the engine is ticking, when the last tick was, and how many rules are erroring.
  2. To retime: edit the rule's thresholds and its check every interval, then Save. Out-of-range values are clamped and the field shows what was stored.
  3. Run now executes the rule immediately, ignoring its interval, and reports what it did.
  4. Disable stops it. Use this when a rule is misbehaving — a disabled rule is a known gap; a silently failing one is not.
Recent runs are listed below the rules with what each did and how many things it acted on.

37.4 If the engine is not running

The status page and the automations page both say so explicitly. The engine is complete without pg_cron — anything with a clock can drive it: 

select public.run_automations();

Point an external scheduler at that every five minutes and the service is fully functional while cron is fixed.

Why you will not get spammed Every action is claimed against a unique (rule, entity, tier) record, so a rule running every five minutes alerts once per escalation tier rather than every five minutes forever. If you see the same incident escalate twice, it has crossed from warn to critical — a real deterioration.

38.3 The CCTV cut-over

/api/cctv/cameras serves the SANRAL registry until Hikvision is enabled with an app key, secret and base URL — then the same endpoint serves the live HikCentral estate. The cut-over is a toggle in Admin, not a deployment. Roll back the same way.

Chapter 39

Branding

Set a department's identity
Screen /admin/departments  ·  Writes department_branding
  1. Choose the department.
  2. Set official name, short name, monogram, motto, primary colour and accent colour.
  3. Upload the crest, or use Clear logo to remove it.
Why crests are uploaded, never shipped Official municipal and service insignia belong to the municipality. Until one is uploaded the interface renders a monogram badge in the department's colours, so nothing looks unfinished. The identity appears in the sidebar, My Schedule, and the mobile shell and profile.
